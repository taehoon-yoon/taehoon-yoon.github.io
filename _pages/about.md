I am a MS student in Artificial Intelligence at [KAIST](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/), advised by [Minhyuk Sung](https://mhsung.github.io/) in the Visual AI Lab. Before that, I received my BS in Physics and Computer Science and Engineering from Sogang University, where I graduated as the valedictorian of the College of Natural Sciences.

My research interests are in **generative modeling** and **probabilistic inference**, including diffusion and flow-matching models, controllable generation, reward alignment, inverse problems, and particle-based sampling methods.
